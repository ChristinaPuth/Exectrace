**About the 82% recall.** Two of the eleven labelled pairs were missed, both
"the CEO, named as the speaker or host on a page about someone else":

- `campfire-customer-cfo.txt`: the model wrote "Camp**ground**" where the
  page says "Campfire" in its quote. The evidence check refused the claim.

page instead (a bug in my check, since fixed) and found Glasgow. So the
model is not fully repeatable even at temperature 0, mainly because two
different models answer depending on pacing. A misquote costs coverage, not
correctness: nothing wrong was stored on any of the five pages, and no
person from another organisation was stored.

## 5. Errors and issues met during M3

| # | What happened | Cause | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The model named people as "Mr. Tenev" and "Ms. Bazzano" | It copied the sentence's wording | A claim needs a given name and a surname; the prompt asks for the full name given elsewhere. The honorific forms are refused and logged |
| 2 | Evidence such as "Director" or "each of our founders..." was accepted for a named person | The check only asked whether the quote was somewhere in the document | The person's surname must be in the quote or within 250 characters of it |
| 3 | Real table rows were refused ("evidence not in the document"), losing Robinhood's chief legal officer | The page text keeps table-cell separators that the model leaves out | Separators are treated as formatting, like whitespace |
| 4 | **Dates were invented.** "since May 2020" came back as 2020-05-01, "since August 2024" as 2024-08-01 | The model completed a month to a day although told not to | A date is kept only when the document writes that exact day; otherwise it is dropped and logged. 3 dates were dropped in the last Robinhood run |
| 5 | A past title was read as current: Shiv Verma's earlier post showed as a current role | The model returned "role" for "Prior to his current position, Mr. Verma had been..." | Prompt rule: a title given as past is a role-scoped departure. He now shows as former in that post |
| 6 | "Dan Gallagher" and "Daniel Gallagher" were two leaders | The same office was spelled with and without a comma, so the roles did not match and the nickname rule did not merge them | Commas and "and" are normalized in the canonical role |
| 7 | "Chief Compliance Officer" of a subsidiary looked like a company-wide office | The title did not carry the unit | A title held in a unit keeps the unit's name and gets no single-holder role |
| 8 | The annual report yielded only its signature page | It refers to the proxy statement for its officers, and my ranking of pieces favoured legal wording | Named offices now count most. The officers are found in the proxy statement |
| 9 | Titles such as "Founder CEO / Co-founder" were not recognised as CEO | A slash was read as naming a division | Fixed; test added |
| 10 | The accuracy run missed the CEO on a page | My proximity check deleted text between quotation marks, and the name stood between two quotations | Fixed; test added |
| 11 | The independent re-check reported 10 false failures | It compared the merged name ("Vladimir Tenev") with pages that write "Vlad Tenev" | The name is now stored as each source wrote it, and the re-check uses that |
| 12 | The model invented a person: "John Locke, CEO" on a Campfire post, and misquoted "Campground" | Model error | Both refused by the evidence check (step 11). This is the check doing its job |
| 13 | Two of my new tests failed on first run | Two runs within one second share a log file, and the tests read the earlier run's lines | The tests read only the lines after the last run start. The shared file itself is harmless |

Issue 4 deserves your attention: without that check the database would have
held plausible-looking dates that no source states.

## 6. Decisions made during M3 that the spec did not fix

Please confirm or change these in manual test MT-3.9.

1. **Extraction is part of `leadership collect`**, in the same run and under
   the same limits. `--no-extract` stops after fetching.
2. **The quote must be near the person's name** (250 characters). New
   requirement R156.
3. **A date must be written in full in the source** to be stored. New
   requirement R157. Month-only dates are lost, not rounded.
4. **A title in a unit of the company** keeps the unit's name and is not a
   company-wide single-holder role. New requirement R158.
5. **The name is stored as each source wrote it**, and
   `tools/check_evidence.py` can re-check every claim. New requirement R159.
6. **R153 is implemented by a page-kind judgment from the model**, applied
   by code: a third-party page judged a profile or directory drops to tier
   4. It can only lower a third-party source. In the final data no page was
   lowered, because the M2 exclusions had already removed such pages.
7. **Long documents are read in part**: at most 3 pieces of 4,500 characters,
   chosen by leadership wording.
8. **Presidents and general counsels are C-level.** Directors, board members
   and founders without an office are stored as `other`, outside the scope.
9. **Location and bio are not extracted in M3** beyond a location the page
   states outright. Enrichment is M4.
10. **Two version stamps.** The model answer is cached by prompt, schema and
    model. A change to the checks or rules re-processes documents from that
    cache with no model call.
11. **A run with no LLM key ends `insufficient_data`**, saying no people
    could be extracted.
12. **Four database columns were added**: `sources.extraction_version`,
    `sources.page_kind`, `people.possible_duplicate_of`, and
    `claims.person_as_written`. Older databases get them on opening.
