## 6. Limitations

- **Coverage is set by what a site lets the tool read.** For Robinhood and
  Wise the official leadership page is closed to the fetcher. The pipeline
  does not find vice presidents or most chief officers there.
- **Verification by search did not settle any role.** It adds third-party
  evidence, which the rules rightly treat as weak.
- **No personal location was found.** Leaders' locations are rarely stated
  in the sources read.
- **Biographies are thin.** Each is one or two quoted sentences. Some only
  restate the title, such as "Kristo Käärmann is the co-founder and CEO of
  Wise." One is old: Daniel Gallagher's comes from a 2020 article. Erik
  Leavell has none, because no source read describes him.
- **Reachable recall depends on what the gold set cites.** A person counts
  as unreachable only if every source the gold set cites was refused. If the
  person is also named somewhere else that was never searched, they are
  still left out. That is why the full figure is always shown beside it.
- **The gold sets are not fully blind.** They were drafted after earlier
  pipeline runs, from primary sources, and approved by you row by row.
- **Recall is low where leadership pages refuse the tool**: Robinhood
  C-level 5 of 11 (45%) and VP 0 of 14 against the full gold set. Discovery
  steps 1 and 4 did not change this (section 11).
- **Two former roles have no end date**, because no source states one.
- **The model is not fully repeatable.** Results moved between runs: the
  Warnick sentence was returned in one run and not in another. The
  model-free recovery and the checks are what keep the stored data stable.
- **Brendan Doyle is missed.** An author line under a blog post is not
  extracted as a leader.

## 7. Decisions made during M4 that the spec did not fix

Please confirm or change these in manual test MT-4.8.

1. **Verification and enrichment are part of `leadership collect`**;
   `--no-verify` stops before them.
2. **A check reads at most three new pages.**
3. **One automatic search pass per company** (your decision). A role is
   checked once and never searched again; once a pass ends, later runs make
   no automatic search. `--refresh` allows another pass.
4. **Stated dates and earlier titles are recovered without a model**, by
   strict sentence rules. Sentences that disagree add nothing.
5. **The title must be tied to the person** (R166), replacing the
   250-character rule for titles. The window still applies to dates and to
   "is the quote about this person at all".
6. **A biography is quoted text**, never a summary written by the model.
7. **The location search is one query per person**, made only when the held
   sources give no location.
8. **`leadership eval` measures the fixtures**, not the working database,
   when fixtures exist. `export` must be run first.
9. **Precision counts only judged roles.** Unjudged and secondary-candidate
   roles are shown and counted apart, never as correct.
10. **A gold row counts toward every group its title names**, and unit-level
    offices never match a company-wide office (your instruction).
11. **A new `checks` table** and a `location_status` column in the view.
12. **Wise's leadership page was read in a browser for the hand-check
    only**, as you did for Robinhood. The pipeline did not read it.
13. **Reachable recall** (your decision): always shown beside full recall;
    a person is left out only on a stored refusal of every source the gold
    set cites for them.
14. **A biography must have the person as its subject** and say what they
    are, were or did. A model-free fallback picks the document's own first
    qualifying sentence.

## 11. Source discovery: steps 1 and 4 built, and what they did

You approved steps 1 and 4 of the plan on 2026-11-13. Steps 2 and 3 are not
built.

**Step 1, role-word searches on the company's own domains.** Three fixed
queries ("vice president"; "general manager" or "head of"; "chief ...
officer" appointed, joins or named), sent with the search limited to the
company's own domains. No domain or name is written into the queries. The
provider's filter is not trusted: any result off the company's domains is
dropped. At most 6 new pages are read. It runs once per company, like every
automatic search; `--refresh` allows it again.

**Step 4, officer sections of long documents.** When a document is too long
to read whole and none of the pieces chosen holds a heading such as
"Executive Officers" or "Leadership Team", one of the three reading slots
goes to the piece under the best such heading. A heading is a short line,
not a sentence. Documents whose officer section was already read are read
exactly as before.

**What was learned for step 3.** On Wise, step 1 found the FY2025 Annual
Report, the UK prospectus and a US registration statement, all PDFs on
`wise.com`. An annual report usually lists the whole executive team. They
were first sent to the model as raw bytes, which wasted model calls and
found nothing. That is now fixed: a PDF or other non-web file is recorded as
skipped, "not read: application/pdf files are not read (only web pages
are)", and never sent to the model. Reading PDFs is step 3, not built.
